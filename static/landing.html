Scene analysis

From a catalogue search to a dated, readable record

Every step of the workflow happens on one screen: search, review, render, compare and export.

A

Guided search with usable results

Choose a product, a date range and an area. BluVerify lists every acquisition with its cloud cover, so the usable scenes are obvious before anything is downloaded.

  • Cloud cover colour-coded per scene: clear, partly cloudy, overcast
  • Rasters rendered on demand and overlaid with a real-value legend
  • Adjustable opacity to check results against the base map
B

Reports written for decision-makers

Every search saves a scene report: one row per date, with readable column names, real units, a data-quality grade and a one-sentence summary of what the satellite measured.

  • Share of your area the satellite could actually see
  • Good, Fair or Poor quality grade for every date
  • Product-specific measurements: vegetation cover, open-water area, gas levels
  • Opens cleanly in Excel, including °C, km² and µ symbols
C

Compare dates to see what changed

Fetch two or more scenes and review them in four synchronised views.

Side-by-side: two synchronised maps with a scene picker on each.

GIS-ready GeoTIFF

Download rasters coloured to match the preview, or as raw 32-bit values. Georeferenced for QGIS and ArcGIS.

Scene report CSV

The plain-language report for every search, saved alongside the rasters for audit and reuse.

Past-run comparison

Merge the reports from earlier searches into one chronological table, including older report formats.

Carbon footprint

District emissions, sector by sector, with the air to match

Search a district by name or draw an area on the map. BluVerify finds the district by real boundary overlap and builds its greenhouse-gas footprint from Climate TRACE.

  • Yearly CO₂-equivalent totals and year-on-year change
  • Breakdown into ten plain-language sectors
  • Named facilities, such as power stations, landfills and airports
  • Satellite NO₂, methane and CO: the last 12 months against the 12 before
  • Emissions and facilities CSVs for your own analysis

How it works

Three steps, straight from the map

No accounts to configure and no files to prepare. The application opens directly to the map.

  1. 1

    Choose a product and dates

    Pick from 15 products, each labelled with what it shows. Presets set common date ranges in one click.

  2. 2

    Draw your area

    Draw a rectangle on the map. Coordinates and area in km² are filled in, and the sidebar flags anything still missing.

  3. 3

    Review, fetch and compare

    Read the scene report, fetch the rasters you need and compare dates. Everything can be downloaded.

Methodology

Transparent about what the data can and cannot show

Optical measurements exclude clouds and cloud shadows, and dates where too little of the area is visible are marked rather than guessed. Air-quality changes within sampling noise are reported as “about the same”.

Screening, not certification Level labels are indicative guides for non-specialists, not regulatory limits, and emissions figures are estimates for screening.
