Two tools, one map

From raw satellite passes to answers you can act on

TerraVerify wraps satellite and Climate TRACE emissions data in a guided interface, so anyone can check what changed on the ground — and what it emits.

Satellite scenes

Search the satellite catalog for any rectangle and date range. Every acquisition is summarised in a plain-English scene report, and any scene can be rendered on demand.

  • Guided three-step search with date presets and AOI area in km²
  • Cloud cover colour-coded so usable scenes stand out
  • Fetched rasters overlaid on the map with a real-value legend
  • Side-by-side, swipe, grid and metadata comparison
  • Merge past runs into one chronological table

Carbon footprint

Search a district by name or draw an area. TerraVerify finds the district by real boundary overlap and builds its greenhouse-gas footprint from Climate TRACE.

  • Yearly CO₂-equivalent totals and year-on-year change
  • Breakdown by sector in plain categories
  • Named facilities — power stations, landfills, airports
  • Satellite NO₂, CH₄ and CO: last 12 months vs the 12 before
  • Is your area higher or lower than its district?
How it works

Three steps. No satellite expertise.

  1. 1

    Pick a product

    Choose from 15 optical, radar, thermal and air-quality products — each labelled with what it actually shows.

  2. 2

    Draw your area

    Set a date range with one click and draw a rectangle on the map. The sidebar tells you what's still missing.

  3. 3

    Read, fetch, compare

    Get a scene list and report CSV, fetch the rasters you need, and compare dates side by side to spot change.

Data products

15 products across 4 satellite missions

Measured over your area by statistical analysis and rendered with consistent, legend-backed colour scales.

Multispectral

Optical imagery

Up to 10 m imagery with cloud and shadow masking, so a cloudy day never reads as “no vegetation”.

  • True Color
  • NDVI
  • NDWI
  • NDBI
  • NDSI
  • EVI
Microwave

Radar (SAR)

Sees through clouds, day and night. Detects likely water and flooding, and strong reflectors like buildings.

  • VV/VH backscatter
Wide-swath

Ocean & land

Wide-area colour imagery and thermal infrared for approximate surface temperature, coolest and hottest spots.

  • OLCI True Color
  • SLSTR thermal
Atmospheric

Air quality

Quality-filtered trace gases in familiar units, with indicative levels and where each gas comes from.

  • NO₂
  • CO
  • CH₄
  • O₃
  • SO₂
  • Aerosol
Plain-language reports

One row per date. One sentence that explains it.

Every search writes a scene report CSV built for non-specialists: readable column names, real units, a data-quality grade and a summary sentence — with technical details kept at the end.

  • Share of your area the satellite could actually see
  • Good / Fair / Poor quality grade per date
  • Product-specific measurements — % dense vegetation, open-water km², gas levels
  • UTF-8 with BOM, so Excel shows °C, km² and µ correctly

Carbon footprint

What does this district emit — and is the air changing?

Emissions come from Climate TRACE, grouped into ten plain categories from power and industry to farming and waste. Satellite air-quality indicators show where burning and methane sources leave a mark.

  • Emissions only — land carbon-balance fluxes are excluded, so year-on-year changes stay honest
  • Changes within sampling noise read as “about the same”
  • Emissions and facilities CSVs, ready for your own analysis
