      How it works

      Three steps, straight from the panel on the left.

      1. 1 Choose a product and dates Pick from 15 products, each labelled with what it shows. Presets set common date ranges in one click.
      2. 2 Choose your area Draw a rectangle on the map or type its coordinates. The area in km² is filled in for you.
      3. 3 Review, fetch and compare Read the scene report, fetch the rasters you need and compare dates. Everything can be downloaded.

      About the data

      Screening, not certification Optical measurements exclude clouds and shadows, and dates with too little visible area are marked rather than guessed. Level labels are indicative guides, not regulatory limits, and emissions figures are estimates for screening.
